Mise Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 (updated for built-in AI features) · cookmise.app

Mise (“we”, “us”) is a recipe, meal-planning and shopping app. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address, a password (stored and verified by Firebase Authentication — we never see it), and the display name you choose.
  • Content you create: recipes (saved, edited, imported or created), your weekly plan, shopping list and checked items, pantry and staples, recently-made history, likes, and who you follow.
  • Photos you provide: recipe photos you upload, and photos of grocery receipts you scan. Receipt photos are sent for reading and are not kept; only the grocery items you choose to add are saved to your pantry.
  • Basic technical data: standard server and security logs (such as IP address and device/browser type) handled by our hosting and database providers.

What other people can see

Your recipes are private unless you choose “Make public.” Public recipes are visible to other signed-in Mise users together with your display name, and can be liked and saved by them. Your email address, week plan, shopping list, and pantry are never shown to other users. You can unshare a recipe at any time.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign-in, syncing your cookbook, plan and list across devices, and Community features (sharing, likes, following).
  • To provide features you ask for: importing recipes from links, searching for recipes, and AI features (meal plans, nutrition estimates, video/post import).

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) stores your account and data.
  • Vercel hosts the app at cookmise.app.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): when you use an AI feature (smart import, meal plans, nutrition estimates, receipt reading), the relevant content — for example a recipe, the text of a page or post you imported, your meal-plan preferences, or a receipt photo — is sent from our server to Anthropic to generate the result. We do not send your name or email. Anthropic’s handling of that data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Links you import: when you import a link (a recipe site, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube), our server requests that public page — and its photo — on your behalf, so the site sees our server’s address rather than yours.
  • Recipe search: public recipe databases (TheMealDB, TheCocktailDB) and USDA FoodData Central receive the search term you enter, and may be reached through a public relay service (corsproxy.io) where direct access is blocked.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account), or at cookmise.app/delete-account.html. Deletion permanently removes your account, your recipes (including public ones), plan, shopping list, pantry and settings. A like you gave to someone else’s recipe may remain as part of that recipe’s anonymous like count. Backups and logs held by our providers are overwritten on their normal schedules.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and protected by Firebase security rules so that only you can read or change your private data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Mise is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your recipes and settings in the app, unshare public recipes, and delete your account. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, portability); contact us and we’ll help.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for material changes we will notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@cookmise.app

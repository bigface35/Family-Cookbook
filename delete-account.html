Delete your Mise account

cookmise.app · Account & data deletion

You can permanently delete your Mise account and the data associated with it at any time.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Mise and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings (in the account line near the top).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account…
  4. Enter your password and confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, display name, sign-in)
  • All recipes you created or saved, including any you shared publicly in Community
  • Your weekly plan, shopping list, pantry, staples, recently-made history and following list
  • Your stored settings and preferences

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. A like you gave to another person’s recipe may remain in that recipe’s anonymous like count. Provider backups and logs are overwritten on their normal schedules.

Can’t sign in?

Email support@cookmise.app from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my Mise account.” We’ll verify it’s you and delete your account and data within 30 days.

See also our Privacy Policy.
